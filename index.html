How the opportunity score is calculated

A transparent, reproducible formula — not a black box.
Opportunity Score = (normalised demand) × (quality factor from avg rating) × (0.4 + 0.6 × (1 − normalised supply)) × 100.
Logic: more learners = more demand; higher ratings = proven appetite for the topic; fewer existing courses = less competition. This is exactly the kind of analysis a Solution Expert would run before recommending a new training product.
