How the opportunity score works

A transparent, reproducible formula.
Score = (normalised demand) × (quality from avg rating) × (0.4 + 0.6 × (1 − normalised supply)) × 100.
More learners = more demand · higher ratings = proven appetite · fewer courses = less competition.
